Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about visiting our Spiritual Store Collingwood — hours, directions, readings, and gifts.

What are your store hours?

Awaken Spiritual Store in Collingwood is open Tuesday to Saturday, 12:00–5:00 pm. Sunday and Monday are closed.

How do I find Awaken Spiritual Store on Hurontario Street?

We are at 207 Hurontario St Unit 2 in downtown Collingwood. Look for Tracy’s Nails on Hurontario, then the laneway and the Awaken sign for Unit 2. Full north and south driving directions are on our contact section.

Do you offer tarot card readings?

Tarot card readings and other in-store spiritual healing support may be available. Availability and scheduling can change, so please call (705) 293-8811 or ask in store for the current reading schedule.

Can I browse open tarot and oracle decks in the shop?

Yes. Many tarot and oracle decks are kept open so you can feel the cards and find a deck that resonates before you buy. Staff are happy to help if you are new to cards.

What kinds of spiritual products and gifts do you carry?

Our Spiritual Store Collingwood offers healing crystals, tarot and oracle cards, incense and smudging supplies, crystal jewellery, meditation supplies, spiritual books, and mindfulness gifts. Stock rotates, so visit in person to see what is currently available.

Which areas do you serve?

Collingwood is our home and primary community. We also welcome guests from Blue Mountains, Thornbury, Wasaga Beach, and across Southern Georgian Bay who visit our downtown crystal shop and metaphysical store.

Do you sell spiritual gifts for beginners?

Yes. Awaken welcomes beginners with approachable spiritual gifts — including beginner tarot cards, oracle cards for beginners, starter crystals, incense, and mindfulness gifts. Staff are happy to help you choose without pressure.

What is the difference between tarot cards and oracle cards?

Tarot decks usually follow a traditional structure (Major and Minor Arcana) with established card meanings, which many readers appreciate for layered insight. Oracle cards are more free-form — deck size, themes, and messaging vary by creator — and often feel intuitive for beginners. At Awaken you can browse both tarot and oracle decks, including open decks you can feel in person.
