What is the difference between tarot cards and oracle cards?

Tarot decks usually follow a traditional structure (Major and Minor Arcana) with established card meanings, which many readers appreciate for layered insight. Oracle cards are more free-form — deck size, themes, and messaging vary by creator — and often feel intuitive for beginners. At Awaken you can browse both tarot and oracle decks, including open decks you can feel in person.
