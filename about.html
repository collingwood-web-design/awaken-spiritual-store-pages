About Our Spiritual Store Collingwood

Awaken Spiritual Store first opened its doors to the Collingwood community in June of 2018 as a spiritual store and metaphysical shop rooted in care. Penny, the owner, believes in healing the healer. In her experience, customers bring as much to her as the store gives to them. It’s an energetic exchange. Often, customers are looking for permission to “simply be”.

Penny is someone who gives you permission to be your authentic self so you can learn, heal, grow and shine. As we do this, we connect to our higher selves. When we connect, we ultimately align with the Divine. As Penny will teach us, perfection lies in the divine, setting us up to live our best lives — whether you visit for healing crystals, tarot card decks, oracle cards, or spiritual gifts.

Make a Commitment to Yourself

Our Crystal Shop Collingwood is all about community. It’s a safe place to grow spiritually, explore metaphysical supplies, and embrace the collective energy of Awakening. Neighbours from Blue Mountains, Thornbury, Wasaga Beach, and Southern Georgian Bay are always welcome.
